Multimedia

7.1 Introduction to Multimedia

Multimedia is the combination of different types of media such as text, graphics, audio, video, and animation to present information in an interactive and attractive way. It is used in education, entertainment, advertising, business, games, and communication.

7.2 Components of Multimedia

The main components of multimedia are text, graphics, audio, video, and animation. Text provides written information, graphics provide visual information, audio provides sound, video shows moving images, and animation creates the appearance of movement.

7.3 Text

Text is used to display written information in multimedia. A text editor is used to create and edit text. Important text properties include font type, font style, font size, formatting, and color. Typography is the art of arranging text to make it clear and attractive.

7.4 Graphics

Graphics are visual images used to communicate information. Bitmap graphics are made of pixels and may lose quality when enlarged. Vector graphics are made using mathematical shapes and can be resized without losing quality. 2D graphics have height and width, while 3D graphics have height, width, and depth.

7.5 Audio

Digital audio is sound stored and processed in digital form. MIDI stores musical instructions rather than actual sound. Common audio formats include WAV, MP3, AU, and RealAudio. MP3 provides compressed audio with smaller file size, while WAV generally provides higher-quality uncompressed audio.

7.6 Video

Digital video is the recording and presentation of moving images in digital form. A video is made up of individual pictures called frames, which are displayed rapidly to create motion. Common video formats include MPEG, AVI, MOV, and MPG.

7.7 Animation

Animation is the technique of creating the appearance of movement by displaying images or objects in sequence. Animation tools are used to create moving graphics, presentations, advertisements, and games. Animated GIF is a simple image format that supports multiple frames, while Flash was a multimedia technology formerly used for web animations and interactive content.
